Writing and sharing a bit again here:

* I re-built this site on IndieWeb protocols! It used to be a lot more complicated to maintain but it's now super simple and integrates a lot of architectural ideas and protocols I love and will expand on: [Micropub](https://indieweb.org/Micropub), [POSSE](https://indieweb.org/POSSE) and [PESOS](https://indieweb.org/PESOS). It is nice to think in a place that is shape-able and not tethered to any other platform, but can link with all of them.

It didn't really have any definition. It's probably whatever it feels like to you. I wonder if something like that is still out there. Probably have to post to find out.
