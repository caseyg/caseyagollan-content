I had heard it would be good, but I had no idea how life-giving [@jennitaur](https://social.coop/@jennitaur) new book “Saving Time” would be: https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/672377/saving-time-by-jenny-odell/

It’s got the tangential/soft focus of a daydream, the satisfaction of following deep internet/historical rabbitholes to their ends, and the devastating funniness of those moments where you realize that paradigms are paradigms and can’t ever unsee it again.

A much needed reminder: scientific management is not a law of nature, but a contingent genealogy.
