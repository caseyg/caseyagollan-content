<p>Podcasts are *the ideal format* for GenAI slop bc they’re (by and large) so meandering, oftentimes just background noise, mostly carried by occasional spurts of armwaving and overemoting.</p><p>In the dominant paradigm of generating concise summaries or bulleted key points, there’s something jarring about gaps or misses. </p><p>But in an AI podcast injected with such a high percentage of filler (umm, ahh!, ooh, nailed it!) wheels of otherwise squeaky plot lines and argumentation are greased and forgiven.</p>
