    html: <p>I really do stand by the idea of just ... not making a formal organization. Not everything has to be a corporation, LLC or 501(c)(3). Usually you don't need legal liability separation or any of the things a legal entity brings.</p><p>Mostly you just need a responsible point of contact for any services you use, and a bit of a succession plan.  And if you keep both minimal, there's not much there there.</p>
